# PSYCHE-delic: Field Guide to Your Own Mind

## Overview
**PSYCHE-delic** is a digital cabinet of self-observation exercises designed to help individuals explore their perceptions, dreams, and inner experiences. It offers a collection of exercises—ranging from simple reflections to playful activities—aimed at fostering self-awareness. The platform emphasizes personal introspection over clinical diagnosis, encouraging users to spend quiet moments engaging with their own thoughts and emotions.

> *"Psyche-delic is a cabinet of self-observation exercises — perceptions, dream reports, fun and games. Not for diagnosis, few quiet minutes instead."*

## Key Features
- **Interactive Exercises**: Users can pull a "Psyche Tile" to open a case and engage with a self-observation exercise.
- **Personalized Responses**: Responses are saved in a user's account for future review and reflection.
- **No Automated Analysis**: All content is designed for individual interpretation; no automatic analysis or psychological evaluation occurs.
- **Account Management**: Users must create an account to save and track their responses.

## User Experience Flow
1. **Access the Hero Section**  
   Upon landing, users are greeted with a visually engaging hero section featuring an illustration of a brain half-made of flowers, symbolizing the intersection of mind and creativity.

2. **Open the Cabinet**  
   A prominent "Open the cabinet" button invites users to begin exploring the available exercises.

3. **Authentication**  
   To save responses, users must:
   - **Create an Account** (via email and password)
   - **Log In** (existing users)
   - A clear note explains that account creation enables saving and reviewing responses over time.

4. **Engage with Exercises**  
   The "Psyche Tiles" section presents a catalog of exercises. Each tile can be pulled to open a case, revealing a unique self-observation prompt.

## Design and Tone
- **Visual Style**: Clean, calming, and artistic, with soft colors and intuitive layout.
- **Tone**: Warm, inviting, and non-judgmental—emphasizing exploration over outcomes.
- **Intended Audience**: Individuals seeking personal insight, mindfulness, or creative self-expression.

## Accessibility and Privacy
- **Privacy Focus**: No automated analysis or data sharing. Responses remain private and are intended for personal reflection.
- **No Diagnostic Use**: The platform is explicitly not intended for diagnosing mental health conditions.
